Know Your Rights

Right to Equality

Every person is equal before the law and deserves equal protection.

Right to Freedom

Citizens have important freedoms protected by the Constitution.

Right to Education

Children aged 6 to 14 have the right to free and compulsory education.

Right against Exploitation

The Constitution prohibits human trafficking and forced labour.

Free Legal Aid

Eligible people can receive free legal services through the National Legal Services Authority (NALSA) and State Legal Services Authorities.
